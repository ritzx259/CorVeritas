Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The reference guided the sunset colors and composition; the live interface and animated heart are implemented in React and CSS.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 16:9 website hero background for CorVeritas, a cardiovascular AI research app. Create an original cinematic photorealistic CGI landscape of calm reflective water at sunset. Near-black aubergine and deep plum sky at the top third, a vibrant glowing horizon of dark rose, crimson red and burnt orange across the middle, a warm amber center glow at the horizon, and detailed glossy dark burgundy ripples reflecting orange and gold across the bottom third. Horizon at roughly 52% of image height. Very refined luxury editorial website art direction, low camera over water, subtle atmospheric haze, striking lighting, rich shadows and realistic water. Symmetrical landscape with central warm glow; leave center unobstructed for a live HTML heart sculpture, and dark quiet bottom-left and top-right regions for live website text. Image is a scene asset only, not a mockup. No card, no heart, no product, no arch, no typography, no letters, no UI, no borders, no people, no watermark. Landscape 16:9.
